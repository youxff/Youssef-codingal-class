Amazon Rainforest

The largest tropical rainforest, spanning Brazil, Peru, Colombia, and other countries, home to over 390 billion trees and countless species of plants and animals.

Congo Rainforest

The second-largest rainforest, rich in biodiversity and home to gorillas, elephants, and numerous endemic species.

Daintree Rainforest

Known for its ancient flora and the cassowary, one of the world’s most dangerous birds.
